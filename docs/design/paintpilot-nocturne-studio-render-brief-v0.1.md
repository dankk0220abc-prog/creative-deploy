## 2. Purpose

Generate the next desktop and mobile visual concepts for the PaintPilot Product Entry only.
The concepts should establish cinematic product understanding, a single visual subject,
controlled scroll-story rhythm and one clear transition into the Projects Workspace.

Do not combine the Product Entry with `/paintpilot/projects`. The render is not a Project
dashboard, a production screenshot or evidence that PaintPilot business capabilities exist.
Any concept image produced from this brief is not a formal product asset and must not be committed
as an implementation asset without a separately reviewed asset task.

## 3. Shared Art Direction

- Premium, dark, professional and mysterious Nocturne Studio atmosphere.
- Extremely low information density and strong intentional whitespace.
- One non-character visual subject with clear spatial hierarchy.
- Deep graphite-blue surfaces; never pure black.
- Low-saturation teal magnetic particles, old-gold secondary light and limited terracotta
  material accents.
- Warm-white primary text and gray-blue secondary text.
- Content and visual changes follow a clear Capture / Structure / Direct / Plan rhythm.
- Motion surrounds content and never crosses primary text or the CTA.
- The entry contains one dominant action: `Enter Workspace`.
- Product understanding comes before technical spectacle.
- Do not imitate or reuse reference-site code, assets, branding, layouts or 3D models.
- Do not imply that upload, AI analysis, Polygon editing, RAG, inventory or planning is live.

Candidate palette remains inherited from the Nocturne Studio visual-direction document and is
not implemented or frozen as code.

## 4. Desktop Hero

### 4.1 Format

- Aspect ratio: `16:10`
- View: straight-on full desktop viewport
- Surface: PaintPilot Product Entry at `/paintpilot`
- Density: cinematic, extremely spacious, deliberately limited

### 4.2 Required Composition

1. Compact CreativeDeploy / PaintPilot brand relationship.
2. A very large `PaintPilot` title.
3. One short product-value statement.
4. One dominant `Enter Workspace` CTA.
5. One non-character `Abstract Material Study`.
6. Magnetic particles concentrated around the visual subject.
7. A small number of Polygon nodes and region boundaries.
8. One restrained light-direction line and controlled shadow division.
9. Minimal `PaintPilot is under active development` boundary.

Do not show Project Cards, Create Project, project status, management filters, analytics,
application navigation or a feature grid.

Candidate value statement:

`Plan repaint workflows with visual understanding and human control.`

The copy remains a review candidate.

## 5. Abstract Material Study

The single visual subject should feel like a material research object rather than a character
or product mascot.

Allowed visual ingredients:

- anonymous sculptural volumes;
- resin, plaster or model-material surfaces;
- image-region contours;
- restrained Polygon nodes;
- light-direction and shadow-zone lines;
- low-saturation pigment blocks;
- knowledge, inventory and Human Review signals used as abstract states.

The subject must not be identifiable as:

- a person or character;
- a robot or mecha;
- a specific figure;
- an anime or game property;
- a commercial or unauthorized IP.

It must remain the same visual subject across the four narrative states. Do not replace it with
multiple floating cards or four unrelated illustrations.

## 6. Desktop Narrative Preview

The concept may show a restrained lower-edge preview or a separate supporting board for:

`01 Capture` → `02 Structure` → `03 Direct` → `04 Plan`

Each stage contains only:

- one number;
- one short title;
- one short explanation;
- one core change to the shared visual subject.

State changes:

- **Capture:** dispersed particles focus and an input boundary appears.
- **Structure:** candidate regions and confirmed Polygon boundaries become distinct.
- **Direct:** light direction, hard-edged shadow and pigment zones change.
- **Plan:** nodes resolve into structured steps, Citation / inventory signals and Human Review.

This preview must not become a feature-card wall, timeline dashboard or row of four equal
product modules. The Hero remains the primary concept.

## 7. Final CTA State

The narrative conclusion may be shown as a separate supporting state:

`Enter PaintPilot Workspace`

- Destination: `/paintpilot/projects`.
- It does not directly create a project.
- It does not show project previews.
- It retains the active-development boundary.
- It is the same primary task as the Hero CTA, not a competing action.

## 8. Mobile Hero

### 8.1 Format

- Visual viewport width: approximately `390 px`
- Single-column composition
- No horizontal overflow

### 8.2 Required Composition

1. Compact CreativeDeploy / PaintPilot context.
2. Large PaintPilot title.
3. Short product-value statement.
4. One large `Enter Workspace` CTA.
5. Simplified Abstract Material Study below or beside the text.
6. Reduced particles, blur, texture and depth.
7. Short active-development boundary.

Do not show project lists, cards, Create Project, dense navigation, a fixed complex bottom bar
or desktop-scale 3D interaction.

### 8.3 Mobile Narrative Implication

- Text precedes each simplified visual state.
- Capture, Structure, Direct and Plan remain in one DOM and reading order.
- No interaction depends on hover, free rotation, drag precision or continuous parallax.
- CTA remains clear without crowding the first viewport.
- Reduced motion can show four static subject states with short fades or no transition.

## 9. Motion and Accessibility Cues

Static concepts may imply:

- particles settling toward the Abstract Material Study;
- limited depth between text and the visual subject;
- one controlled change per narrative stage;
- a soft depth transition toward the Projects Workspace.

Do not imply:

- high-speed particle fields;
- large continuous rotation;
- scroll-jacking;
- automatic sound;
- flashing or color-only state changes.

The composition must remain understandable with motion removed. Text and CTA render before
complex visuals, and the visual subject may fall back to a static material silhouette.

## 11. Review Checklist

- Is the concept unmistakably the `/paintpilot` Product Entry rather than a dashboard?
- Is PaintPilot understood before the visual effect is admired?
- Is there one dominant Enter Workspace action?
- Is the Hero extremely spacious with one clear visual subject?
- Is the Abstract Material Study anonymous and non-character?
- Are Project Cards, Create Project and management navigation absent?
- Does the visual language suggest Capture / Structure / Direct / Plan without a feature wall?
- Are AI suggestion and Human-confirmed state visually distinguishable?
- Are particles and depth kept away from primary text and the CTA?
- Does mobile remain clear, single-column and free of horizontal overflow?
- Is a reduced-motion static interpretation obvious?
- Is the active-development boundary visible?
- Does the concept avoid implying implemented AI, Polygon, RAG, inventory or planning?
